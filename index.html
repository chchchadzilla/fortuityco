Executive Summary

A high-level assessment of fortuityco.com across SEO, technology, UX design, and agentic AI readiness.

Assessment Overview

Fortuity Inc. operates a private advisory and fractional family office practice serving ultra-high-net-worth families, their businesses, and trust-held estates. The firm's website — built on WordPress with Divi 5.7.1 — projects understated sophistication with a dark hero section, gold-accented typography, and a deliberate air of exclusivity. However, this analysis reveals significant untapped opportunities in agentic AI integration, technical SEO, client-facing digital experiences, and operational automation that, if addressed, would position Fortuity as a category-defining firm in the modern family office landscape.

The site currently functions as a digital brochure — aesthetically competent but strategically passive. In 2026, where AI agents autonomously process K-1s, reconcile multi-entity portfolios, and generate real-time client intelligence, a static WordPress site without structured data, client portals, or intelligent automation is a competitive liability. This report maps every gap and presents a prioritized roadmap to close them.

SEO & Technical Audit

Deep analysis of on-page SEO, structured data, performance, and search visibility factors.

Critical

No Meta Description on Any Page

The homepage title is "Fortuity Inc. | Putting Family Back Into the Family Office" — decent, but the site has no meta description tag anywhere in the source. Google will auto-generate snippets from page content, resulting in unpredictable, often incoherent search previews. For a firm that values "discretion," having Google choose your words is the opposite of control.

Critical

Zero Structured Data / Schema Markup

The site contains no application/ld+json blocks whatsoever. Missing: Organization, ProfessionalService, Person (team members), FAQPage, and BreadcrumbList schemas. This means Google cannot programmatically understand what Fortuity is, who works there, or what services they offer. Rich search results, knowledge panels, and "People Also Ask" features are entirely inaccessible.

Warning

Exposed WordPress Infrastructure

The site exposes /xmlrpc.php (pingback endpoint), RSS feeds, oEmbed JSON/XML endpoints, and a wp-json REST API. The generator meta tag reveals "Divi Child Theme By The 20 v.1.0" — giving attackers precise version intelligence. For a firm handling UHNW families' sensitive data, this surface area is unnecessarily large.

Warning

No Open Graph / Social Meta Tags

When the site URL is shared on LinkedIn, Twitter/X, or messaging apps, there are no OG tags to control the preview card — no image, no description, no title override. For a referral-driven business, this is a missed branding opportunity every time a client or partner shares a link.

Note

Performance Overhead: Divi Framework

The page ships 19+ JavaScript files and massive inline CSS blocks (the Divi parent stylesheet alone is ~3,500+ lines inlined). Combined with jQuery shims, mediaelement player, carousel libraries, and waypoint/animation scripts, the homepage has significant render-blocking overhead. For a site that is primarily text and imagery, this is disproportionate. Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, INP) will be negatively affected.

UX, Design & Layout Analysis

Evaluation of the visual identity, information architecture, user experience, and conversion pathways.

What's Working Well

Brand Voice & Copywriting. The site's tone — "Discretion does not advertise" — is exceptionally well-crafted. The copy avoids generic jargon and communicates exclusivity with restraint. This is a genuine competitive asset.

Color & Typography System. The navy (#2c2d3b) / gold (#b4975a) palette with Times New Roman headings projects old-world authority. The full-viewport hero with video background and dark overlay creates an immediate premium impression.

Critical Design & UX Issues

Critical

No Client Portal or Secure Login

The site offers zero post-engagement digital touchpoints. Clients who have entrusted their family's entire financial architecture to Fortuity have no way to log in, view documents, track progress, or communicate securely through the website. In 2026, competitors offer AI-powered consolidated dashboards, secure document vaults, and real-time entity-level reporting. Fortuity's clients are likely using email and phone — a security and convenience gap.

Warning

One-Dimensional Navigation Architecture

The footer reveals a rich service taxonomy (Tax, Assets, Wealth, Estate, Business) but the primary navigation buries these under "Our Practice." The site lacks clear user journeys for different personas — a serial entrepreneur exploring business consulting vs. an aging patriarch evaluating estate continuity should have different entry paths.

Warning

Static "Insights" Section — No Content Engine

The /insights/ page exists but functions as a static article archive. There's no categorization by service area, no search functionality, no related content suggestions, no newsletter capture, and no thought leadership distribution strategy. For establishing E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust) — Google's core quality signal — this is a major missed opportunity.

Warning

Mobile Experience Degradation

Custom JavaScript menu system ("fortuity-hamburger") overrides Divi's native mobile nav. While this shows intentionality, the multi-view system switches content layouts at 980px and 767px breakpoints with different text formatting — the phone layout for the hero tagline stacks each phrase vertically without the gold divider's impact. The CTA button group shifts to a column layout that may push key actions below the fold.

Note

Conversion Path Analysis

The site has exactly two conversion endpoints: "Schedule an Appointment" and "Explore Our Practice" — both buttons in the hero. The /connect/ page is the sole intake mechanism. There are no micro-conversions (newsletter signup, downloadable white papers, assessment tools) to capture prospects who aren't ready for a call. For a firm that is "selective by design," this binary approach loses the nurture-stage audience entirely.

Agentic AI Gap Analysis

Agentic AI — What Fortuity Is Missing

The firm currently leverages zero agentic AI capabilities. Here's every gap — and why it matters.

"In 2026, small family office teams of 3–8 professionals are achieving the operational coverage and analytical output previously reserved for teams of 20+ — entirely through AI agents."

Critical Gap

No AI-Powered Document Processing

Fortuity manages "nearly a thousand businesses" and "more than a hundred trust funds." Each entity generates tax filings, K-1s, distribution notices, capital calls, and legal documents. Without agentic AI, staff must manually ingest, classify, and cross-reference every document. Modern agents process entire K-1 packets in minutes — extracting partner allocations, tax attributes, and compliance flags autonomously. Fortuity is doing this by hand.

Critical Gap

No Automated Cross-Entity Reconciliation

With hundreds of families and their businesses, reconciliation across entities, custodians, and jurisdictions is a massive operational burden. AI agents can perform intelligent transaction matching, anomaly detection, and cross-entity reconciliation — reducing month-end close times from weeks to days. The firm's "disciplined, repeatable methodology" is the perfect foundation for AI automation, yet none exists.

Critical Gap

No Client-Facing AI Intelligence Layer

Clients have no dashboard, no real-time portfolio view, no AI-generated summaries of their holdings. When a principal asks, "What are my Q3 distributions across all trusts?" — someone at Fortuity must manually pull data, build a spreadsheet, and send an email. An agentic system would answer that question in seconds, directly from a secure portal, with natural-language explanations.

Missing

No AI-Enhanced Intake or Prospect Qualification

The /connect/ page uses a basic form. An AI-powered intake system could: pre-qualify prospects based on entity complexity, auto-schedule based on the right advisor's expertise, surface preliminary data from public records, and generate a pre-engagement intelligence brief — all before the first human conversation.

Missing

No Proactive Tax-Loss or Opportunity Surfacing

AI agents in modern family offices continuously monitor portfolios for tax-loss harvesting windows, estate freeze opportunities, Roth conversion optimal timing, and regulatory changes. Fortuity's tax and wealth planning services are reactive by default — they wait for review cycles. Agents make these services continuous.

Missing

No AI Content Generation for Thought Leadership

The /insights/ section is sparse. An AI agent could draft regulatory alerts, market commentary, and educational content based on Fortuity's practice areas — then route to a human for review and publication. This feeds SEO, builds authority, and gives the team an "always-on" content engine without hiring a content team.

SEO & Content Strategy Recommendations

Actionable fixes and strategic content initiatives to improve search visibility and authority.

Immediate Technical SEO Fixes

● Immediate — Week 1

Implement Meta Descriptions Across All Pages

  • Homepage: "Fortuity Inc. provides private advisory and fractional family office services — integrated tax strategy, asset preservation, estate architecture, and wealth planning for discerning families."
  • Each service page should have a unique 150-160 character description reflecting its specific value proposition
  • Install Yoast SEO or RankMath to manage at scale
● Immediate — Week 1

Deploy Structured Data (JSON-LD)

  • Organization schema: Legal name, logo, description, contactPoint, address, sameAs (LinkedIn)
  • ProfessionalService schema: Per practice area page — Tax, Wealth, Estate, etc.
  • Person schema: For each team member on /firm/our-people/ with jobTitle and affiliation
  • BreadcrumbList schema: On every interior page for navigation context
  • FAQPage schema: If/when FAQ content is created — triggers "People Also Ask" features
● Immediate — Week 1

Add Open Graph & Twitter Card Tags

  • og:title, og:description, og:image (use branded share image), og:url, og:type
  • twitter:card = "summary_large_image", twitter:title, twitter:description
  • Create a branded OG share image (1200×630px) with Fortuity's lockup + tagline

Content Strategy — Building Authority

● Ongoing

Thought Leadership Content Pillars

  • Regulatory Intelligence: Tax law changes, estate planning updates, compliance alerts — showing the firm is always watching
  • Family Governance: Succession planning frameworks, next-gen education, governance best practices
  • Business Owner Advisory: Entity structuring, exit planning, valuation insights for serial entrepreneurs
  • Discretion & Philosophy: Thought pieces on the family office model — this plays to Fortuity's strongest brand differentiator
● Ongoing

Long-Tail SEO Keyword Strategy

  • "fractional family office services" — low competition, exact match
  • "family office tax strategy for entrepreneurs"
  • "trust fund management advisory"
  • "multi-entity business structure optimization"
  • "estate continuity planning for business owners"
  • "family office for digital asset entrepreneurs"
  • Each keyword should anchor a dedicated content piece or landing page

Security Hardening Recommendations

Security

Reduce WordPress Attack Surface

Disable XML-RPC (/xmlrpc.php), remove the generator meta tag revealing "Divi Child Theme By The 20 v.1.0," restrict REST API endpoints to authenticated users, and implement Content Security Policy (CSP) headers. For a firm handling UHNW client data, the exposed WP infrastructure is a reputational risk.
